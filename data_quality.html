Statutory Authority & Legislation Registry

Income-Tax Act, 1961 vs 2025 Transition
  • • STCG (Listed Equity): Sec 111A (1961 Act) $\to$ Section 196 (2025 Act) @ 20% flat.
  • • LTCG (Listed Equity): Sec 112A (1961 Act) $\to$ Section 198 (2025 Act) @ 12.5% above ₹1.25L exemption.
  • • Dividend TDS: Sec 194 (1961 Act) $\to$ Section 393(1) Table Sl. 7 (2025 Act) @ 10%. ₹10,000 threshold applies strictly to resident individuals; Nil threshold for corporates.
  • • No-PAN Withholding: Sec 206AA $\to$ Section 397(2) @ 20% penal rate.
  • • Loss Set-Off & Carry-Forward: Sec 70/74 $\to$ Section 108/111; transition protected under Section 536.
Securities Regulation & Transaction Costs
  • • SEBI LODR 2015: Reg 30 (Material disclosures/auditor turnover), Reg 31 (Promoter encumbrance), Reg 33 (Quarterly financials within 45 days), Reg 42 (Ex-date notice).
  • • STT: Finance (No. 2) Act, 2004 Chapter VII @ 0.1% on delivery buy and 0.1% on delivery sell.
  • • Stamp Duty: Indian Stamp Act, 1899 Schedule I @ 0.015% on delivery buyer turnover only.
  • • GST on Brokerage: CGST Rate Notification 11/2017 SAC 997152 @ 18% strictly on fees & charges (STT and Stamp Duty excluded from taxable value under Section 15(2)(a)).
